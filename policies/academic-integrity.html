Academic Integrity Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This document is a general template for the EduDesign UK website and should be reviewed to ensure it reflects current practice and applicable UK law.

Our role

EduDesign UK provides academic guidance, structured support, research assistance, referencing support, editing, proofreading and demonstration materials to help you develop and improve your own work.

Your responsibility

  • You are responsible for your own submissions and for following your institution's rules.
  • Support materials are intended for reference, learning and improvement.
  • You should not present third-party work as entirely your own where your institution prohibits it.

No guarantees

We do not guarantee grades, and we do not claim to guarantee any specific AI-detection or similarity score. Academic outcomes depend on your own effort, your university's requirements and the marking criteria.

Similarity & originality

We can provide similarity-report guidance and originality review using the checking method available to us. We do not imply official affiliation with any third-party system.

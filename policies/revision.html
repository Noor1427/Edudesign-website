Revision Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This document is a general template for the EduDesign UK website and should be reviewed to ensure it reflects current practice and applicable UK law.

What is included

Revision options depend on the service and the scope agreed before work begins. The number and type of revisions are confirmed in your quotation.

Revisions vs new work

A revision refines the agreed deliverable. Requests that change the original brief, scope or length are treated as new work and quoted separately.

Requesting revisions

Please request eligible revisions promptly after delivery, with clear, specific feedback so they can be actioned efficiently.

Academic work

For academic services we provide guidance, feedback and supporting materials to help you improve your own work, rather than completing submissions on your behalf.

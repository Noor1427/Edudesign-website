Feedback & Review Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This document is a general template for the EduDesign UK website and should be reviewed to ensure it reflects current practice and applicable UK law.

We publish only genuine feedback, reviewed before it appears on the website.

  • Reviews must reflect genuine experiences.
  • Feedback may be verified.
  • Personal details may be anonymised.
  • Submissions are reviewed before publication.
  • Abusive, fraudulent or unrelated content will not be published.
  • Customers may request removal of their displayed feedback.

How to submit or remove feedback

Use the feedback form in the Feedback section. To request removal of published feedback, contact us via the contact section.

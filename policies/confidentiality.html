Confidentiality Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This document is a general template for the EduDesign UK website and should be reviewed to ensure it reflects current practice and applicable UK law.

We take confidentiality seriously across academic, personal and business work.

  • Personal data is handled privately.
  • Student numbers and university identifiers are not displayed.
  • Business data and source files are not publicly shared.
  • Portfolio publication requires permission or anonymisation.
  • Relevant samples may be shown privately where permitted.

Requests

To discuss confidentiality or request that information be removed, contact us via the contact section.

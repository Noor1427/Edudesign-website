Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This document is a general template for the EduDesign UK website and should be reviewed to ensure it reflects current practice and applicable UK law.

Project-based work

Because work is tailored to each request, refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis according to the stage of the project and the agreed scope.

Before work begins

If you cancel before work starts, any amount paid (minus non-refundable processing where applicable) is returned.

After work begins

Once work is underway, amounts covering completed work are non-refundable. Remaining, unstarted portions may be eligible for a partial refund.

Quality concerns

If a deliverable does not match the agreed scope, contact us first — eligible revisions (see the Revision Policy) are provided before any refund is considered.

Requesting a refund

Send your request via the contact section with your project details.
